2. Manual 48-Hour Triage

Scientific Methodology

Deterministic Multi-Dimensional Risk Formula

Replacing subjective guesswork with transparent, peer-auditable mathematical models.

Risk Score (0–100) = Hazard Score (0–1.0) × Exposure Factor (0–1.0) × Vulnerability Index (0–1.0) × 100
Hazard Score (H)

Wind speed (scaled against 250 km/h) + surge height (slosh model) + water inundation head above ground level.

Exposure Factor (E)

Distance to storm eye cone + elevation mask (< 5m MSL) + critical grid and highway dependencies.

Vulnerability Index (V)

Structural classification (concrete vs masonry) + mission criticality (ICU > substation) - active mitigation measures.
